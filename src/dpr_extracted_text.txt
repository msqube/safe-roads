Background and Need NKDA is a statutory authority under the Government of West Bengal responsible for development and maintenance of New Town, Kolkata. The road covered here is Road NKDA MAR 1111 , which has four potholes with a total area of 20 sq ft that need filling before resurfacing. Resurfacing with strengthening is proposed to restore riding quality, structural capacity and drainage-shedding profile of the carriageway, and to extend pavement life. Project Objectives Restore a smooth, durable wearing course on Road NKDA MAR 1111 . Strengthen the pavement through an overlay (strengthening layer plus BC surfacing) so that it carries the design traffic for the design period. Improve road safety and ride comfort for residents, commuters and service vehicles. Reduce recurring maintenance cost through a one-year defect liability backed by security deposit. 4. The approach below is the standard practice for such works and must be confirmed against the approved BOQ and specifications (MoRTH Specifications for Road and Bridge Works, relevant IRC codes, and MORD/SP 20/SP 72 as applicable). Pavement evaluation: visual condition survey, and where required Benkelman Beam Deflection or core cutting, to set overlay thickness. Surface preparation: patch repair of potholes and failed areas, removal of loose material, cleaning and drying of the surface. Profile correction / strengthening layer: levelling course as required by the BOQ to correct camber and level. Tack coat: uniform application on the prepared surface before the BC layer. Bituminous Concrete: hot-mix produced at a Batch Mix Plant, transported covered, laid with a paver and compacted with tandem/pneumatic rollers within the specified temperature window for the bitumen viscosity grade. Finishing: edge treatment, joints, adjustment of manhole and chamber covers, road marking. Quality control: tests on aggregates, bitumen, mix design (Marshall properties), field density, thickness and surface regularity at the frequencies in the contract and the MORD specification/SP 20/SP 72, with test results reported to the Engineer-in-Charge. Minimum site resources: all necessary tools and plant, a laboratory with minimum testing equipment, and a site supervisor who is either a Diploma Civil Engineer with 3 years’ experience or a graduate Civil Engineer with 1 year’s experience . 7. Operation, Maintenance and Defect Liability The defect liability period is one year from the date of completion. During this period the contractor makes good any defect or damage at its own expense. After handover, NKDA should include the streets in its periodic maintenance programme , covering crack sealing, pothole patching, marking refresh and drain cleaning, so that the BC surface reaches its intended life. Conclusion The project is a routine but time-critical package of pothole filling and BC resurfacing for Road NKDA MAR 1111 with an estimated cost of Rs. 2.82 crore, to be completed in 30 days. It is technically straightforward, with a 10% security deposit and a one-year defect liability as safeguards. The main delivery risks are plant capacity for the short timeline and weather.
